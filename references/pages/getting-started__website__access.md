# Access

## Website address​

The HH Development portal is accessible to everyone with a HH Development user account.

The website address is the following: hh-dev.com

The portal is accessible via the Log In link at the top right of the screen.

![](https://help.hhtiming.com/assets/images/access_1-2188744a730ae6a3e906386827d1b25c.png)

## Website Login​

On the website, the user will be prompted with the sign-in page:

![](https://help.hhtiming.com/assets/images/access_2-b9cf3616ac496f32744b87c8c0c88222.png)

Each user should have received an introduction email following account creation with the information on how to setup a password.

Forgot your password?
From the website ( hh-dev.com) login page, please click on Forgot Password and follow the instructions on the screen:

![](https://help.hhtiming.com/assets/images/access_3-2233c3d8aa7eb3d837d10b480d4aa860.png)

Then type your Username (email) and click Submit:

![](https://help.hhtiming.com/assets/images/access_4-a9d511327a4c5ea7ab0bedbc700b74e3.png)

An email will then be sent to your account with a link to reset your password.

## Logout​

To log out of the website, the user can click on his initial on the top right corner of the website and click on Logout:

![](https://help.hhtiming.com/assets/images/access_5-57706b27cee9bb02de62f2a708b14b3e.png)

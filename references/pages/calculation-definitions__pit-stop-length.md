# Pit stop length

Pit stop lengths are used in the race finish [https://help.hhtiming.com/calculation-definitions/race-finish-calculation] and future stints calculations for both fixed [https://help.hhtiming.com/calculation-definitions/fixed-stint-length] and predicted [https://help.hhtiming.com/calculation-definitions/predicted-stint-length] stint length types. By default the time used for future pit stops is the average of all the previous green flag stops on a per car basis. In most scenarios this is appropriate however in some situations a user may prefer to manually enforce the pit stop times.

A manual pit stop time can be input and forced in the session settings [https://help.hhtiming.com/settings-options/backstage-options/#session-settings].

# FAQ

### What is HH Timing?​

HH Timing is a timing, scoring and strategy software.

### Who are HH Development?​

HH Development is an international company that combines motorsport experience with software and engineering.

### Where do I find timing data?​

This is usually available from the series or timing provider's website. You can contact us if you are having trouble finding the data you are looking for.

### Which series is HH Timing compatible with?​

We are constantly adding championships to our series. If the series you are interested in is not listed below get in touch with us about getting it added. As long as timing data is available, it is likely we can add it to our supported series.

- WEC
- IMSA
- ELMS
- Asian Le Mans Series
- Formula E
- Indycar
- GTWC Europe
- GTWC America
- GTWC Asia
- Intercontinental GT Challenge
- Australia Supercars
- Australian GT
- NLS
- ADAC GT Masters
- Creventic 24h Series
- GT Open
- Super GT
- Super Formula
- StockCar Brazil
- British GT
- BTCC

### Who are your customers?​

Our customers range from private teams and freelancers to manufacturers and series organisers.

### How much does HH Timing cost?​

Contact us [https://help.hhtiming.com/support/email] to discuss pricing. When you lease the software all updates as well as email and phone support are included. We also accommodate reasonable feature requests as part of the base rental price.

### How does the licensing work?​

Licences are leased on a 12 month basis. You can use a single license on multiple computers within a team and across multiple championships without having to purchase multiple licenses.

### Can you develop confidential tools?​

We do build exclusive features and tools for our customers. These are typically implemented via our Plugin Framework which allows development that is not included in the public release. The plugin Framework [https://help.hhtiming.com/plugins/making-your-own-plugin] is open so that if customers have capability in developing software they can develop their own plugins without having to pay HH Development to do so.

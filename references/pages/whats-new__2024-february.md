# 2024 February Release

# 2024 February Release

February 12, 2024 · 2 min read

The February 2024 release of HH Timing brings exciting improvements, many of which were requested by you! Thank you for continuously sharing your feedback and ideas. This release also includes numerous bug fixes for improved stability.

Plugin
This version of HH Timing introduces breaking changes for plugins. We have released new plugins alongside this version, and all plugins require updating.

As always, if you have any questions or issues with the release, please contact us at support@hh-dev.com.

## Gap graph​

In 2024, one of our goals is to consolidate tools that have grown over time. One such tool is the gap graph. For the past few years, we have maintained both a regular gap graph and a more advanced projected gap graph. We have decided to discontinue the regular gap graph and focus on improving the usability of the projected gap graph.

For those who are unfamiliar with it, you can read the help section on gap graph [https://help.hhtiming.com/displays/graphing/gap-graph/]

## Track loops export​

Certain protocols, including Alkamel v2, IMSA Enhanced RMon Timing, and TimeService TeamStream, offer additional list of track loops in addition of the regular sectors and pit loops. To access this functionality, navigate to the DATA/Sessions menu, select your desired session, and then click on the "Copy Track Loops" option [https://help.hhtiming.com/tips_tricks/copy-loops-list/].

The loops are then copied to your clipboard.

Those loops can then be used to create additional sectors.

## New protocol​

The Perugia timing protocol [https://help.hhtiming.com/timekeeper-specific-info/perugia-timing/] was added on this version.

## Car off track​

One control that become more popular in the last year was the car off track display [https://help.hhtiming.com/displays/car-off-track/]. We have re-worked the help file to explain all options.

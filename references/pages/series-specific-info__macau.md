# FIA GT World Cup Macau - 2026

## Timing provider​

The timing at the FIA GT World Cup Macau will be provided by ITS Chrono [https://help.hhtiming.com/timekeeper-specific-info/its-chrono].

If you which to receive the timing feed, you will have to contact ITS Chrono to get an API Key.

## Championship configuration​

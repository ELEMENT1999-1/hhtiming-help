# Driver scoreboard

All drivers are listed in this scoreboard. Here is a quick way to understand the relative performance and driving time of drivers across the whole field.

![](https://help.hhtiming.com/assets/images/driver_1-842b895c6b2d0f18e02236391d3724ed.png)

## Scoreboard Export​

The main scoreboard can be automatically exported to an external Excel workbook [https://help.hhtiming.com/excel-exports/scoreboard-live-excel-export] to be used for external calculations.

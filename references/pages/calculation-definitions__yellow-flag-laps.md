# Yellow flag laps

The condition for determining whether given lap has a yellow status, as seen in the car scoreboard [https://help.hhtiming.com/displays/scoreboards/car-scoreboard] are:

- Safety Car
- Full Course Yellow
- Slow Zone
- Code 60
For a lap for a given car to be considered yellow, one of the above conditions must be true at the end of that lap (when the car crosses the finish line).

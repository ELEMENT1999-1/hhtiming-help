# Paying with credit card

caution
This section of the website is only accessible by Account Admin users.

- Login to our website using the same credentials that are used to retrieve licenses and software downloads.
- Go to Billing under the Admin section

![](https://help.hhtiming.com/assets/images/cc_1-85a393c82163081070555b844af76579.png)

- Insert the invoice number and the amount and click Make Payment

![](https://help.hhtiming.com/assets/images/cc_2-100c05970f58ac790e786ccf5c19148f.png)

- Enter the credit card information to finish the payment

![](https://help.hhtiming.com/assets/images/cc_3-f0fcb5f733cfadd5cdafa9165fc68aaa.png)

# Assumed pace

At the center of a number of the calculations used within HH Timing is the “Assumed Pace” assumption. To make forward predictions about the current and future stints a lap time must be assumed. This lap time is defined as the average of the best 3 laps in the last 5 laps.

![](https://help.hhtiming.com/assets/images/assumed_pace_1-08c0e744f0fa9c586401c80515995295.png)

Note that the above calculation for Assumed Pace ignores in/out laps and any “yellow” laps such as Safety Car and Full Course Yellows when selecting the last 5 laps.
